#  Perfume Sales Analysis Dashboard
 Project Overview

The Perfume Sales Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics project developed to analyze perfume sales performance, revenue generation, customer purchasing behavior, brand performance, and branch-wise sales distribution.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales data, helping businesses understand sales trends, identify top-performing brands, evaluate customer segments, and compare sales across different branches and sales channels.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making and improve overall sales performance.

Project Objectives

Analyze total net sales and gross sales.

Monitor the total number of units sold and transactions.

Identify top-performing perfume brands.

Analyze monthly sales trends.

Compare sales performance across branches.

Understand customer purchasing patterns by age group.

Evaluate online and in-store sales performance.

Monitor profit margins and sales performance.

Identify business growth opportunities.

🔍 Key Business Insights

Overall Sales: Total net sales reached 108.64 million AED.

Units Sold: Approximately 455,360 perfume units were sold.

Customer Transactions: The business recorded approximately 392,800 transactions.

Leading Sales Branch: Nesto generated the highest branch sales at 23.63 million AED.

Customer Segment: Customers aged 26–35 contributed the highest sales, approximately 45.48 million AED.

Sales Channel: In-store sales generated 97.72 million AED, significantly higher than online sales of 10.93 million AED.

Brand Performance: Ajmal recorded the highest brand sales at approximately 16.11 million AED.

Gross Sales: Total gross amount reached 110.71 million AED.
